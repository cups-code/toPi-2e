# Reflections
> [!Abstract] Definition
> Reflections are a type of transformation, allowing for the graph to be flipped across a line to create a mirror image on the other side. 

Reflections are similar to [[8.04 + 8.05 - Dilations|dilations]], since it is, with respect to the function $y=k f(x)$, $k=-1$.

> [!Note]
> In Mathematics Advanced, only reflections about the x and y-axis will be explored.
## Vertical Reflections
$$
y=-f(x) \
y->-y
$$
Vertical reflections are reflections about the x-axis. This is when every y-value is flipped.

Let $y=x^3$.

| x   | 0   | 1   | 2   |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| y   | 0   | 1   | 8   |

Now, reflect $y=x^3$ vertically. This should now return as $y=-(x^3)=-x^3$.

| x   | -2  | -1  | 0   | 1   | 2   |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| y   | 8   | 1   | 0   | -1  | -8  |


> [!Tip]
> In vertical reflections, the y-intercept of the function CAN change. For instance, let $f(x)=x^3+1$. The y-intercept of this function is (0, 1). If it was vertically reflected ($-f(x)$)…
> $$
> f(x)=x^3+1 \
> -f(x)=-(x^3+1) \
> = -x^3-1
> $$
> The y-intercept is now (0, -1).

## Horizontal Reflections
$$
y=f(-x)\
x->-x
$$
Horizontal reflections are reflections about the y-axis. This is when every x-value is flipped.

Let $y=x^3$.

| x   | 0   | 1   | 2   |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| y   | 0   | 1   | 8   |

Now, reflect $y=x^3$ horizontally. This should now return as $y=(-x)^3$. Thus, its values are the same (since it is an even function).

| x   | -2  | -1  | 0   | 1   | 2   |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| y   | 8   | 1   | 0   | -1  | -8  |
> [!Tip]
> Horizontal reflections CANNOT change the y-intercept because the y-values do not change (only the x-values do).
