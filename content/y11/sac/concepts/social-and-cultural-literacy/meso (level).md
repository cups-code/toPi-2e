The level between [[micro (level)]] and [[macro (level)]] society. It consists of certain [[institutions]] such as communities, schools, neighbourhoods, church groups, workplaces, sport, and recreational teams at a branch and state level. It consists of larger-scale groups that are larger than groups in [[micro (level)]] society. It examines the relationships between the individual and mid-level social structures ([[institutions]]).
