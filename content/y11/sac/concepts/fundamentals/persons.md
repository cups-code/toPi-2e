The concept of *persons* refer to the idea that every individual is unique, and develops in an environmental and social setting where they are influenced by, and also, engages in interactions with other persons and groups.

These major interactions include [[communication]] and cooperation. As a result of these interactions between all [[#Micro, Meso, and Macro|levels of society]], their identity is achieved.
