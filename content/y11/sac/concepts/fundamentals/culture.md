Culture is defined as the shared knowledge, and attitudes and behaviours that gives each [[#Society|society]] its own identity, coherence, and distinctive way of life. Through **norms**, **values**, **customs**, **beliefs**, laws, **rules**, arts, artefacts, governance, and technologies, it demonstrates culture and is used to interpret *meaning* and solve worldly present and future issues.

Culture is not static; it undergoes change, and is dynamic across [[#Time|time]].
