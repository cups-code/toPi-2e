# Reflections
> [!Tip] Note
> Reflections are similar to [[8.04 + 8.05 - Dilations|dilations]], since this is basically (with respect to the function $y=k f(x)$) $k=-1$.
## Vertical Reflections
$$
y=-f(x)
$$
## Horizontal Reflections
$$
y=f(-x)
$$
## Combined Reflections
$$
y=-f(-x)
$$
