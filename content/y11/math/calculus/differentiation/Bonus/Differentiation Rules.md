# Differentiation Rules
## Rule 1
The derivative of a constant is zero.

> [!Example]
> If $f(x)=k$, then $f'(x)=0$. If done graphically, observe the fact that the gradient does not change across every x-value.

## Rule 2
If $f(x)=x^n$, then $f'(x)=n x^(n - 1)$.

> [!Tip]
> This was covered in [[6.03 - The Derivative of xⁿ]] in more detail.
