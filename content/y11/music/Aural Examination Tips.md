## NESA Verbs for Music
- Outline – indicate the main features
- Explore – to look into closely; examine
	- e.g. if explore duration, list subtopics of duration, then explain
- Describe – provide characteristics and features
- Explain – make the relationships between things evident; provide why and/or how
- Analyse – identify components and the relationship between them
- Compare – show how things are similar or different

## Example Question

> [!Question]
> Compare how tone colour is used in Pachelbel’s *Canon in D* and Maroon 5’s *Memories*.

| Canon in D                                                            | Memories                                                             |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Tone colour**: Bright, vibrant, petite, twinkling, warm, deep, dark | **Tone colour**: Synthetic, warm, powerful, mellow                   |
| *Violins* playing in arco bring warm, bright, and vibrant sound       | *Male, tenor vocals* using chest voice bring warm but powerful sound |
| *Violin* playing in pizzicato are twinkling and petite                | *Guitar* being plucked bring synthetic and artificial sound          |
| *Cello* playing in arco give deep sound                               | *Backing male vocals (oohs)* in chest voice bring mellow sound       |
> [!Abstract] Teacher’s Guidance
> **Differences Between Each Piece** 
> For Canon in D:
> - It has a warm, mellow, resonant tone colour.
> - There are strings and a harpsichord.
> - It has an acoustic tone colour.
> 
> For Memories:
> - It has a synthetic, mellow, uplifting, muffled tone colour.
> 	- This tone colour is influenced by the reverb effect on the vocals.
> 	- The uplifting tone colour is influenced by the plucking of the guitar.
> - It is the contemporary pop genre.
> - There are vocals, electric guitar, electric bass, and synthesiser.
> 	- It has a studio-produced tone colour.	  
> 
> **Similarities Between Each Piece**
> - Both gradually add layers, creating a warmer, grander tone colour. It transitions from homophonic to polyphonic.
> - Both of them use the same repeated harmonic progression, which create a calm, nostalgic sound.
> - Pizzicato in both the strings in Canon in D and the bass in Memories create the uplifting tone colour.

> [!Tip] Further Guidance
> As you’ve noticed, this question will ask of you to explain the instruments involved within both pieces (texture) and how the sound is created (expressive techniques), even if it is a tone colour question.
