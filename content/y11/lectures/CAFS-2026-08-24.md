# Exam Technique and Strategies
- Timing
	- Look at time you allocate to each section
- Mark allocation
	- 2 mins per mark
	- 1 min for each multiple choice !!
- Lined paper, space provided –>(general) guide
- Doing multiple choice can help you feel success
	- Building confidence, rhythm
- Spend 10 mins breaking down questions (planning)
- “A lot easier to get a 3/4 in the last 2 mins when you’re really tired than it would for you to pull a 5/8 or 6/8”
- “Where are you putting your energy?”
- “They \[multiple choice] are easy marks if you get it right”
- 2 choices that are quite similar
- One makes it correct
- One makes it incorrect
- Not jumping at you straight away –> **DO PROCESS OF ELIMINATION!!!!!**
- Mindfulness before & after exam
	- “I know some people in here are anxious *examers*”
	- Take a second to yourself
	- 5 mins of silence before exam
	- Decompress after exam, avoid asking questions about the exam
