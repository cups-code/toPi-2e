The concept of environment is defined by the notion that every society resides in a particular physical setting, interacting with its *physical* environment. Locations and its environments – urban, rural, isolated, inland, and coastal – affect society’s opportunities, as well as their constraints. The values and attitudes people hold in relation to their environment greatly affect the interactions between [[#Society|society]], [[#Persons|persons]], [[#Culture|culture]], and most importantly, the [[#Environment|environment]].
