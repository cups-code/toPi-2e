The sense of self. Perceived from either a personal, cultural, or social level, it is formed through time and is the result of [[micro (level)]], [[meso (level)]], and [[macro (level)]] societal interactions. It involves layers or dimensions, creating a sense of inclusion in a group or culture.

There are various factors that contribute to one’s identity, which may include [[gender]], sexuality, beliefs, class, family, ethnicity, social status, group membership, and national pride.
