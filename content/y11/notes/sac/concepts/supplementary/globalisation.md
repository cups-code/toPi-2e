The process of integration and sharing of services, goods, capital, knowledge, sport, leisure, ideas, and culture between countries. Through [[technologies]], globalisation has been more evident in society, through…
- the rise of global patterns of consumption and consumerism,
- the rise of global sport,
- the growth of transnational corporations,
- the widespread increase of world tourism,
- and the growth of global economic systems and military.
